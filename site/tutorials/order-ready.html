HYPOTHETICAL SCENARIO

It is late afternoon. The team is juggling the counter and collection orders. A customer asks about her cake. The answer is already in the order system; the conversation needs to reach it.

The aim is to keep service moving: receive the question, check the order and share an update. This is a possible use, not a promise of more sales or time savings.

Start with the connection ↗

A CONVERSATION IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

Two paths. A clear purpose for each.

RECEIVE · IN

Someone writes in WhatsApp. Bridge brings the new message to RELAYERS. RELAYERS forwards it to the application you chose.

SEND · OUT

The application requests a reply. RELAYERS passes that request to Bridge. The linked WhatsApp sends to the authorized conversation.

Receiving and sending are separate actions. An incoming message can reach your application; a reply depends on an action or a rule you set up there.
01

STEP 1

Prepare one conversation first.

Follow the first-connection guide to link RELAYERS and Bridge. Authorize a test conversation and both receiving and sending. Choose the order system that will take part.

Bridge creates IN and OUT separately.
02

STEP 2

Bring the question to the order system.

In RELAYERS, forward IN to your application. A new question in the chosen conversation can then enter the order’s service workflow.

Linking the person to their order belongs to your application. Bridge does not find orders by itself.
03

STEP 3

Decide who answers and when.

The team can check the order and request a reply. If the system offers an order-ready notification rule, that rule can request the send through OUT.

The rule and wording must be set up in your system. Installing the extension does not start automatic replies.
04

STEP 4

Check the reply in WhatsApp.

Bridge receives the send request and the authorized WhatsApp processes it. Check delivery in the conversation and keep a human path for questions the system cannot handle.

An accepted send does not prove the person received or read the message.

THE LOCATION IS YOUR CHOICE

Here at your business or somewhere else?

◉RELAYERS→Your local application

The application runs on your business computer. Open RELAYERS Desktop there and sign in to the same account to link it. In Bridge, select that computer and the receiving address supplied by the application. Keep Desktop connected during use.

Meet RELAYERS Desktop ↗
◎RELAYERS→Application elsewhere

The application runs on a server or is used by a team elsewhere. In Bridge, choose an online application and enter its receiving address. RELAYERS forwards messages to it; reply requests use the authorized sending path.

In either case, WhatsApp and Bridge remain on the linked computer. A remote application does not need RELAYERS Desktop running on that other machine; its service must be reachable.

THE EXPERIENCE YOU ARE PREPARING

More context for the team. A conversation that continues for the customer.

The system can run in the shop or elsewhere. People work where their information is, while the business WhatsApp remains the conversation channel.

  • The customer agreed to receive order updates.
  • The order is linked to the right conversation.
  • The system defines the wording and timing.
  • A person is available for exceptions.

You choose the account and conversations. Only newly observed messages in those conversations enter this flow; Bridge does not import your entire history.

“Accepted by WhatsApp” means the app accepted the send request. Check delivery in WhatsApp. An uncertain result calls for checking before another attempt.
