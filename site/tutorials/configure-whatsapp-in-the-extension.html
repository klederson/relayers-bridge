HYPOTHETICAL SCENARIO

In the story of a small shop, the team wants order questions to reach its support system. The person responsible wants to choose conversations, separate receiving from sending and check conditions before the connection starts.

Bridge’s configuration screen brings these choices together. Sign in to RELAYERS, authorize conversations and prepare receiving and sending separately. Then use words of interest or combine conditions without writing code. Your application remains responsible for deciding or preparing the reply.

A CONVERSATION IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

Two paths. A clear purpose for each.

RECEIVE · IN

Someone writes in WhatsApp. Bridge brings the new message to RELAYERS. RELAYERS forwards it to the application you chose.

SEND · OUT

The application requests a reply. RELAYERS passes that request to Bridge. The linked WhatsApp sends to the authorized conversation.

Receiving and sending are separate actions. An incoming message can reach your application; a reply depends on an action or a rule you set up there.
01

STEP 1

Open “Configure WhatsApp” in the extension panel.

Click Bridge’s browser icon. Below the RELAYERS brand, use “Configure WhatsApp” to open the connection page. If your account is not linked yet, sign-in and registration links are available there.

The controls in this guide require extension version 0.2.0 or later. Check the installation note before starting.
02

STEP 2

Link your RELAYERS account using the code.

Click “Connect to RELAYERS”, allow the requested connection and open “Open RELAYERS to approve”. Sign in, approve the displayed code and return to Bridge. Conversation selection appears after approval.

Sign in to RELAYERS ↗Create an account ↗
Approving Bridge links the browser to your account. Enter your password on the RELAYERS page.
03

STEP 3

Choose conversations and allowed directions.

Select the WhatsApp account and the conversations for your case. Enable receiving, sending or both and read the authorization. The filters below respect that account and conversation selection.

A filter cannot authorize another conversation. Start with someone who agrees to help test.
04

STEP 4

In Basic mode, enter words of interest.

Under “Receive messages”, choose “Basic” and enter, for example, “order, quote”. A new message passes if it contains either phrase. You can set a separate list under “Send messages”. Case and accents are ignored.

An empty list permits all new messages in authorized conversations. The outgoing filter only allows or blocks a request; it does not write or create replies.
05

STEP 5

In Advanced mode, combine conditions for your routine.

Choose “Advanced”, add conditions and decide whether all must match or just one. For example: the text contains “order” and does not contain “cancelled”. For receiving, you can also choose the message kind. Conditions can apply to one authorized conversation.

Use “Try the filter without sending” to check an example text. It simulates a text message in the first authorized conversation and does not send messages.
06

STEP 6

Choose where the application receives messages.

For an online application, select that option and enter its supplied receiving address. For a local application, choose a computer linked to your RELAYERS account and the address provided by its owner. That computer’s RELAYERS Desktop or receiving connection must stay available.

“Receive in RELAYERS · choose an application later” keeps receipt in the service without preparing application forwarding. The connection summary shows that distinction.
07

STEP 7

Create the connection and check both paths.

After reviewing authorization, click “Create connection”. Bridge creates the IN receiving and OUT sending paths you selected. Sending is linked to this Bridge. If you provided a receiving application, its path is prepared too. Under “Integration details”, copy the receiving and sending addresses separately.

Incoming messages are filtered before leaving the extension. A sending request that fails its filter is blocked before WhatsApp sends it.
08

STEP 8

Edit carefully and resume when ready.

For an existing connection, click “Configure webhooks and filters”. Review conditions and destination, check the authorization and save. The connection stays paused for your review. Click “Resume” to continue. If saving fails, correct the configuration before resuming.

New incoming filters apply to newly observed messages. Already queued messages retain their original authorization. Sending remains limited to the authorized conversations.
09

STEP 9

Check a complete example with your application.

Send a test message matching the receiving filter and confirm it reaches your application. Then request a reply that matches the sending filter and check WhatsApp. The application can be on another machine; the WhatsApp computer and Bridge still need to stay available.

The guide’s test used real RELAYERS and simulated WhatsApp. Captures contain fictional data and show the interface, not a real customer service deployment.

THE LOCATION IS YOUR CHOICE

Here at your business or somewhere else?

◉RELAYERS Desktop→Your local application

The application runs on your business computer. Open RELAYERS Desktop there and sign in to the same account to link it. In Bridge, select that computer and the receiving address supplied by the application. Keep Desktop connected during use.

Meet RELAYERS Desktop ↗
◎RELAYERS→Application elsewhere

The application runs on a server or is used by a team elsewhere. In Bridge, choose an online application and enter its receiving address. RELAYERS forwards messages to it; reply requests use the authorized sending path.

In either case, WhatsApp and Bridge remain on the linked computer. A remote application does not need RELAYERS Desktop running on that other machine; its service must be reachable.

THE EXPERIENCE YOU ARE PREPARING

One receiving path. One sending path. Conditions chosen by you.

Your team can see what is authorized and where the application takes part. Receiving, deciding and sending stay distinct, with configuration controls inside the extension.

  • The RELAYERS account was linked.
  • Conversations and directions were authorized.
  • Basic or advanced conditions were reviewed.
  • The receiving destination matches the chosen application.
  • After saving an edit, the connection was reviewed and resumed.

You choose the account and conversations. Only newly observed messages in those conversations enter this flow; Bridge does not import your entire history.

“Accepted by WhatsApp” means the app accepted the send request. Check delivery in WhatsApp. An uncertain result calls for checking before another attempt.

✓

What was checked

On 9 November 2026, we used a test account on the RELAYERS service and simulated WhatsApp. The test checked sign-in and approval, separate connections, receipt in an application and a reply through the sending path, preserving the WhatsApp page. This was not delivery to a person in a real signed-in WhatsApp session.
