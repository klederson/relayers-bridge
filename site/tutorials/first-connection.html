HYPOTHETICAL SCENARIO

Picture a small bakery. Bia receives “Is my order ready?” in WhatsApp. She wants the message to reach her order system instead of hunting for the answer in several places. When the order is ready, that system could request a reply through the same business WhatsApp.

Bridge brings the conversation closer to the work already happening in your business. RELAYERS organizes the path between WhatsApp and your chosen application. What to answer remains a decision for your team or the rules in your application.

How to install the test package ↗

Before you start

  • A RELAYERS account.
  • Bridge installed and WhatsApp Web open in the account you will use.
  • A test conversation with someone who agrees to take part.
  • An application chosen to receive messages and request replies.

A CONVERSATION IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

Two paths. A clear purpose for each.

RECEIVE · IN

Someone writes in WhatsApp. Bridge brings the new message to RELAYERS. RELAYERS forwards it to the application you chose.

SEND · OUT

The application requests a reply. RELAYERS passes that request to Bridge. The linked WhatsApp sends to the authorized conversation.

Receiving and sending are separate actions. An incoming message can reach your application; a reply depends on an action or a rule you set up there.
01

STEP 1

Choose what you want to connect.

Start with one test conversation. Keep WhatsApp Web signed in and open in the browser where Bridge is installed. Choose an application: an order system, a support desk or another tool your business already uses.

Bridge does not need to be installed on every team member’s machine. It stays in the browser holding the WhatsApp session.
02

STEP 2

Sign in to your RELAYERS account.

Use “Sign in to RELAYERS” in this guide. If you do not have an account, choose “Create an account”. Use the same account to organize the Bridge connection and the path to your application.

Sign in to RELAYERS ↗Create an account ↗
Signing in lets you manage your account. Authorizing the extension is the next step.
03

STEP 3

Approve Bridge using the displayed code.

Click the extension icon. Use “Configure WhatsApp” below the RELAYERS brand to open the connection page. Click “Connect to RELAYERS”. Bridge shows a code and “Open RELAYERS to approve”. Open that button, sign in if asked and approve the code. Then return to Bridge.

Enter your password on the RELAYERS page. You do not need to paste your password into the extension.
04

STEP 4

Choose the account, conversation and both directions.

In Bridge, select the WhatsApp account and test conversation. Enable “Incoming messages → RELAYERS” and “RELAYERS → outgoing messages”. Read the authorization and continue below to choose filters and destination before creating the connection.

You can allow only one direction. For this first two-way example, choose both.
05

STEP 5

Find the receiving and sending paths.

Bridge creates two separate addresses in your RELAYERS account: one for messages received from WhatsApp (IN) and one for send requests (OUT). Find them in “Integration details”. In the RELAYERS panel, look for “WhatsApp Bridge IN” and “WhatsApp Bridge OUT”.

In the current flow, Bridge creates these addresses for you. There is no field for attaching addresses created earlier in the panel. The sending link back to this Bridge is also created automatically.
06

STEP 6

Choose where your application takes part.

On the Bridge page, choose Basic or Advanced filters. Under “Where should received messages go?”, choose your online application or a linked computer and enter its supplied receiving address. Review your choices and click “Create connection”. Use the local and remote options explained below.

Bridge prepares the receiving and sending paths you selected. If you choose receipt only in RELAYERS, application forwarding is left for later; the connection summary makes that clear.
07

STEP 7

Complete a conversation with a test participant.

Ask the person to send “Is my order ready?” in the authorized conversation. Check the message in RELAYERS and in your application. Then use the sending action prepared in your application to reply “Your order is ready for pickup”. The application uses the connection’s OUT address and sending authorization.

Your application needs that sending action configured. Installing Bridge does not create automatic support or a reply button inside every system.
08

STEP 8

Keep the necessary pieces available.

The WhatsApp computer stays on, with the browser, session and Bridge available. For a local application, RELAYERS Desktop also remains connected. For a remote one, its service remains reachable. You can pause the connection in Bridge.

A remote team can request replies without operating the WhatsApp computer. Sending still depends on the linked WhatsApp being available.

THE LOCATION IS YOUR CHOICE

Here at your business or somewhere else?

◉RELAYERS Desktop→Your local application

The application runs on your business computer. Open RELAYERS Desktop there and sign in to the same account to link it. In Bridge, select that computer and the receiving address supplied by the application. Keep Desktop connected during use.

Meet RELAYERS Desktop ↗
◎RELAYERS→Application elsewhere

The application runs on a server or is used by a team elsewhere. In Bridge, choose an online application and enter its receiving address. RELAYERS forwards messages to it; reply requests use the authorized sending path.

In either case, WhatsApp and Bridge remain on the linked computer. A remote application does not need RELAYERS Desktop running on that other machine; its service must be reachable.

THE EXPERIENCE YOU ARE PREPARING

The message arrived. The information found its place. The reply came back.

In the bakery story, the customer does not need to know where the order system runs. The business chooses where to work, while its linked WhatsApp remains the place to have the conversation.

  • RELAYERS and Bridge are linked through the same account.
  • The chosen conversation appears in the authorization.
  • The new message reaches the application through IN.
  • The application’s requested reply comes back through OUT.
  • WhatsApp keeps its page and draft without reloading to send.

You choose the account and conversations. Only newly observed messages in those conversations enter this flow; Bridge does not import your entire history.

“Accepted by WhatsApp” means the app accepted the send request. Check delivery in WhatsApp. An uncertain result calls for checking before another attempt.

✓

What was checked

On 9 November 2026, we used a test account on the RELAYERS service and simulated WhatsApp. The test checked sign-in and approval, separate connections, receipt in an application and a reply through the sending path, preserving the WhatsApp page. This was not delivery to a person in a real signed-in WhatsApp session.
