Your team is elsewhere. Your service stays close.

HYPOTHETICAL SCENARIO

A small business keeps WhatsApp open on an office computer. The person helping with support works from home. She wants messages in the support application and wants to request replies without controlling the office screen.

The team’s workplace and the computer holding the WhatsApp session can be different. RELAYERS links the application to the authorized Bridge.

Start with the connection ↗

A CONVERSATION IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

Two paths. A clear purpose for each.

RECEIVE · IN

Someone writes in WhatsApp. Bridge brings the new message to RELAYERS. RELAYERS forwards it to the application you chose.

SEND · OUT

The application requests a reply. RELAYERS passes that request to Bridge. The linked WhatsApp sends to the authorized conversation.

Receiving and sending are separate actions. An incoming message can reach your application; a reply depends on an action or a rule you set up there.
01

STEP 1

Keep Bridge on the WhatsApp computer.

Install and link the extension in the office browser. Choose the account and conversations the team may work with. Enable receiving and sending and confirm authorization.

Requesting a send from the application does not require remote control of the screen.
02

STEP 2

Bring incoming messages to the team’s system.

In RELAYERS, forward IN to the remote support application. The team signs in there using the access your business provides and follows newly allowed messages.

Bridge does not create a multi-agent support desk. Conversation assignment and access management belong to your application.
03

STEP 3

Request the reply from the application.

The team member writes the reply in the work tool. Its integration requests a send through OUT. RELAYERS passes the request to Bridge and the office WhatsApp sends it.

The application can be in another city. The WhatsApp computer still needs to stay on and connected.
04

STEP 4

Agree on coverage and pauses.

Decide who watches availability and how the team notices an interruption. Pausing Bridge stops new exchanges; resuming continues pending work still available.

Messages during a pause are not recovered as a complete history.

THE LOCATION IS YOUR CHOICE

Here at your business or somewhere else?

◉RELAYERS Desktop→Your local application

The application runs on your business computer. Open RELAYERS Desktop on that computer, sign in to the same account and choose where incoming messages should go. Keep Desktop connected while using this local application.

Meet RELAYERS Desktop ↗
◎RELAYERS→Application elsewhere

The application runs on a server or is used by a team elsewhere. In RELAYERS, choose that application’s receiving address. It can receive messages and request replies without being on the WhatsApp computer.

In either case, WhatsApp and Bridge remain on the linked computer. A remote application does not need RELAYERS Desktop running on that other machine; its service must be reachable.

THE EXPERIENCE YOU ARE PREPARING

Service where your people are. WhatsApp where its session is already open.

Requesting every reply no longer depends on someone sitting at that browser. Your business remains responsible for organizing service and its limits.

  • The team has access to the support application.
  • The application is prepared to receive and request sends.
  • The office computer stays available.
  • The authorized conversations match the intended service.

You choose the account and conversations. Only newly observed messages in those conversations enter this flow; Bridge does not import your entire history.

“Accepted by WhatsApp” means the app accepted the send request. Check delivery in WhatsApp. An uncertain result calls for checking before another attempt.

✓

What was checked

On 9 November 2026, we used a test account on the RELAYERS service and simulated WhatsApp. The test checked sign-in and approval, separate connections, receipt in an application and a reply through the sending path, preserving the WhatsApp page. This was not delivery to a person in a real signed-in WhatsApp session.
