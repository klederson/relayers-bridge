Complete the first guide before starting. Here you change the destination, approve the second computer and test service from there.

Start with the connection ↗

A CONVERSATION IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

Two paths. A clear purpose for each.

RECEIVE · IN

Someone writes in WhatsApp. Bridge brings the new message to RELAYERS. RELAYERS forwards it to the application you chose.

SEND · OUT

The application requests a reply. RELAYERS passes that request to Bridge. The linked WhatsApp sends to the authorized conversation.

Receiving and sending are separate actions. An incoming message can reach your application; a reply depends on an action or a rule you set up there.

On the WhatsApp computer

First complete the first-connection guide with one test conversation. Bridge stays on the computer holding WhatsApp.

  1. Follow the first-connection guide and confirm one desk round trip.
  2. Keep Bridge linked to the RELAYERS account and chosen conversation.
  3. Leave WhatsApp Web open.

03

STEP 3

Approve this desk in RELAYERS.

In the second computer’s desk

Each desk has its own approval and name.

  1. Click “Connect my RELAYERS account”, then “Open approval in RELAYERS”.
  2. Use the same account as the business Bridge and click “Approve device”.
  3. Return to the desk. Note its name in block 01 and copy its receiving address.
✓
Check before continuing

The desk is connected and you have its name and receiving address.

Did the expected result not appear?

A desk approved under another account will not appear in the business Bridge’s list.

04

STEP 4

Change the receiving destination in the business Bridge.

On the WhatsApp computer

Choose the computer using the exact name supplied by the desk.

  1. Click “Configure webhooks and filters”.
  2. Choose “An application on a linked computer” and paste the address supplied by the second computer’s desk.
  3. Click “Find linked computers” and select that desk’s exact name.
  4. Confirm the change, click “Save WhatsApp configuration”, then “Resume”.
✓
Check before continuing

Bridge is Connected and receiving points to the selected computer.

Did the expected result not appear?

A desk elsewhere still uses the linked-computer option. The online-application option is for systems providing a public receiving address.

05

STEP 5

Fill three fields in the remote desk.

Between Bridge and the support desk

Use the same connection that already worked at the business.

  1. Open “Integration details” in Bridge.
  2. Copy IN, OUT and the OUT sending key into the three corresponding fields in the other computer’s desk.
  3. Click “Connect both paths”. Share the key only with someone you authorize to send.
✓
Check before continuing

The desk shows “IN and OUT connected”.

Did the expected result not appear?

Do not create extra addresses in the panel for this move. Use the existing connection.

06

STEP 6

Provide service from the other computer.

In the support desk

Work in the desk without operating the business WhatsApp screen.

  1. Start a short conversation with the authorized participant or bot and wait for a response.
  2. Check that it reaches “Received messages” in the remote desk.
  3. Write the reply there and click “Send reply through WhatsApp”.
  4. Check it in the WhatsApp conversation and follow the other side’s next response.
✓
Check before continuing

The question reached the other computer and its reply returned through business WhatsApp.

Did the expected result not appear?

Keep both computers connected and running. To end the test, pause Bridge and disconnect the desk.

THE LOCATION IS YOUR CHOICE

Here at your business or somewhere else?

◉RELAYERS→Your local application

The application runs on your business computer. Open RELAYERS Desktop there and sign in to the same account to link it. In Bridge, select that computer and the receiving address supplied by the application. Keep Desktop connected during use.

Meet RELAYERS Desktop ↗
◎RELAYERS→Application elsewhere

The application runs on a server or is used by a team elsewhere. In Bridge, choose an online application and enter its receiving address. RELAYERS forwards messages to it; reply requests use the authorized sending path.

In either case, WhatsApp and Bridge remain on the linked computer. A remote application does not need RELAYERS Desktop running on that other machine; its service must be reachable.

THE EXPERIENCE YOU ARE PREPARING

Service where your people are. WhatsApp where its session is already open.

Requesting every reply no longer depends on someone sitting at that browser. Your business remains responsible for organizing service and its limits.

  • Bridge and the desk use the same RELAYERS account.
  • The selected name belongs to the second computer’s desk.
  • IN, OUT and the sending key belong to the same connection.
  • A new message reached the remote desk.
  • Its reply appeared in business WhatsApp.

You choose the account and conversations. Only newly observed messages in those conversations enter this flow; Bridge does not import your entire history.

“Accepted by WhatsApp” means the app accepted the send request. Check delivery in WhatsApp. An uncertain result calls for checking before another attempt.
