HYPOTHETICAL SCENARIO

A customer asks whether Saturday is available. The team uses a booking application and wants the question to reach the right place. Once they agree, the team wants to confirm through the same conversation.

Bring the conversation closer to the schedule your business already uses. Availability, reservations and decisions remain with the application and your team.

A CONVERSATION IN BOTH DIRECTIONS

Two paths. A clear purpose for each.

RECEIVE · IN

Someone writes in WhatsApp. Bridge brings the new message to RELAYERS. RELAYERS forwards it to the application you chose.

SEND · OUT

The application requests a reply. RELAYERS passes that request to Bridge. The linked WhatsApp sends to the authorized conversation.

Receiving and sending are separate actions. An incoming message can reach your application; a reply depends on an action or a rule you set up there.
01

STEP 1

Choose a test conversation.

Link Bridge to your RELAYERS account and allow receiving and sending for an authorized conversation. Choose the application where your team manages appointments.

Start with someone who agrees to test before including everyday service.
02

STEP 2

Bring the question to the team.

Forward IN in RELAYERS to your chosen application. The new scheduling question can enter your booking support workflow.

Bridge does not check availability, reserve a slot or replace the booking system.
03

STEP 3

Send the confirmation you agreed on.

After the team or application confirms the booking, its integration can request a send through OUT: “Your appointment is confirmed for Saturday at 10.”

Reminders are part of the flow only if your application offers them and the person agrees to receive them.
04

STEP 4

Leave room for the person to answer.

A later question or request for a change can follow IN again. The team reviews it and continues the conversation in the application.

The booking change must be completed in your system; a message alone does not update the schedule.

THE LOCATION IS YOUR CHOICE

Here at your business or somewhere else?

◉RELAYERS→Your local application

The application runs on your business computer. Open RELAYERS Desktop there and sign in to the same account to link it. In Bridge, select that computer and the receiving address supplied by the application. Keep Desktop connected during use.

Meet RELAYERS Desktop ↗
◎RELAYERS→Application elsewhere

The application runs on a server or is used by a team elsewhere. In Bridge, choose an online application and enter its receiving address. RELAYERS forwards messages to it; reply requests use the authorized sending path.

In either case, WhatsApp and Bridge remain on the linked computer. A remote application does not need RELAYERS Desktop running on that other machine; its service must be reachable.

THE EXPERIENCE YOU ARE PREPARING

An organized schedule. A conversation someone follows.

The customer keeps using a familiar conversation. The team works in the tool that organizes appointments, with a two-way connection that can support service.

  • The application manages the schedule and knows the booking.
  • The confirmation is tied to the authorized conversation.
  • The person agreed to any reminders.
  • The team follows replies and changes.

You choose the account and conversations. Only newly observed messages in those conversations enter this flow; Bridge does not import your entire history.

“Accepted by WhatsApp” means the app accepted the send request. Check delivery in WhatsApp. An uncertain result calls for checking before another attempt.
