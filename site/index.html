01 / MESSAGES GO BOTH WAYS.

What arrives in WhatsApp can go to RELAYERS.
RELAYERS can send back through WhatsApp.

Turn on receiving, sending, or both. Nothing starts until you choose the chats and give permission.

Receive ↘WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A message arrives.
RELAYERS can receive it too.

Someone sends you a new message. If it is in a chat you allowed, the extension can take its text to RELAYERS.

WhatsApp→Bridge→RELAYERS
AN EXAMPLE YOU CAN SET UP

A customer asks for help. The message can arrive in RELAYERS and be passed to the service you choose.

Only new messages in the chats you choose. It does not fetch your whole history or download files.
Send ↗WHAT YOU SEND

RELAYERS asks to send.
Your open WhatsApp sends it.

Your program can ask RELAYERS to send a message, even from somewhere else. The extension receives that request and sends through the WhatsApp already open on your computer.

RELAYERS→Bridge→WhatsApp
AN EXAMPLE YOU CAN SET UP

An order is ready. Your program asks to send “Your order is ready for pickup” to the customer’s allowed chat.

Once you allow it, you do not need to press Send each time. A send being accepted does not prove the person received or read it.

ⓘThe extension connects the two sides. You choose and set up what RELAYERS should do with the messages.
